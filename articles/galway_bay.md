# Galway Bay case study

## Full demonstration forthcoming

A detailed case study has been developed to demonstrate the application
of `MultiscaleSCP` to an illustrative conservation planning scenario in
Galway Bay, Ireland.

The example integrates conservation features, planning units, and costs
across multiple H3 resolutions, applies multiscale optimisation, and
compares conservation planning outcomes across alternative approaches.

The full demonstration, including R code and detailed results, will be
available once the accompanying paper is published.
